# KOSMION — Warum nicht gerade nach oben?

Complete German documentary, 19:36, Full HD, 25 fps, H.264/AAC.

The release contains one complete MP4 with the supplied narration. See SOURCES.md for full visual attribution and licensing, QUALITY_REPORT.md for completed verification and metadata-de.md for German publication copy.

The film explains inherited orbital motion, the cost of changing direction, the Ulysses Jupiter gravity assist and the spatial environment from the heliosphere to the Milky Way. Original visualizations compress sizes, distances and time; they are not mission ephemerides. Scientific reconstructions are not represented as archival camera footage.
