# KOSMION — Solar System documentary: visual sources

The supplied German voice recording is preserved. Source soundtracks are excluded. Original drawings are illustrative: sizes, distances and time are compressed, and circular inclined tracks are not numerical Ulysses ephemerides. The conditional forty-two-kilometres-per-second example is not a universal minimum for every interplanetary route.

Public-domain NASA material and credited third-party material are distinguished below. Small screen credits are abbreviated; full publisher credits and policies are listed here. Portrait photographs are reframed; no archival image is represented as live mission footage.
